Fuente: https://www.alexstrekeisen.it/english/pluto/antiperthite.php

## Descripcion fuente
Phase diagram of the alkali feldspar system which exhibits such exsolution behavior at low temperatures. At high temperatures the diagram shows that albite (Ab) or NaAlSi 3 O 8 and orthoclase (Or) or KAlSi 3 O 8 form a complete solid solution series. At temperatures just below the solidus, alkali feldspar solid solutions are stable. At lower temperatures, along the curve labeled "solvus" the solid solution is no longer stable. In this case the exsolution phenomena occurs below the solidus and so is a "sub-solidus" reaction. From smith & Brown (1981)
