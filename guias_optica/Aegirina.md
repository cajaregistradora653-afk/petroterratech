## PPL (luz polarizada plana)
- Colour and Pleochroism: Usually colourless, gray, pale green or pale brown, darker colours associated with Fe-rich varieties titanaugite is more distinctly coloured from brown/pink to violet.

- Form: Form stubby prisms elongate along the c-axis, basal sections are 4 or 8 sided and show two cleavages at 90.°

- Cleavage: Parallel to {110}, which intersect at 90°.

- Twinning: Simple and lamellar twins and composition planes, which in combination may form a herringbone pattern.

- Alteration: Commonly alter to uralite (a light coloured amphibole), or may alter to serpentine, chlorite, biotite, carbonates and /or other silicates.

- Relief: High positive.

- Distinguishing Features: Inclined extinction, higher birefringence, 2nd order interference colours, low 2V, optically positive in comparison with opx.
