# Sodalita -> `Sodalita_PPL` / `Sodalita_XPL`

Fuente: https://www.alexstrekeisen.it/english/pluto/sodalite.php

## PPL (luz polarizada plana)
- 
Form:
Commonly form euhedral crystals of dodecahedron habit yielding hexagonal sextion

- 
Color:
Colorless to pale gray-blue

- 
Relief:
Moderate negative relief


## XPL (nicoles cruzados)
- 
Birefringence:
Isotropic
Blue soldalite in a syenite. Itabuna Syenite Complex, Fazenda Hiassu, Bahia State, Brazil. From
James St. John

## Confusables (revisar antes de anotar)
Nefelina (anisotropa debil); Sodalita: isotropa (negra en XPL), incolora-azulada en PPL.
