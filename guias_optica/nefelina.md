# nefelina -> `nefelina_PPL` / `nefelina_XPL`

Fuente: https://www.alexstrekeisen.it/english/pluto/nepheline.php

## Descripcion fuente
Colorless hexagonal prisms of nepheline. Caspar quarry, Bellerberg volcano, Ettringen, Germany. Immage from Mindat. Picture by Stephan Wolfsried. .

## PPL (luz polarizada plana)
- 
Color:
Colorless or rich in inclusions (clouded aspect).

- 
Form:
Hexagonal or stubby prismatic section.

- 
Interference colors:
Very weak.

- 
Relief:
Low.
Colorless hexagonal prisms of nepheline. Caspar quarry, Bellerberg volcano, Ettringen, Germany. Immage from
Mindat. Picture by Stephan Wolfsried.
.

## XPL (nicoles cruzados)
- 
Interference colors:
Very weak.


## Confusables (revisar antes de anotar)
Cuarzo (uniaxico); Sodalita (isotropa). Nefelina: incolora, relieve moderado-bajo, grises I orden.
