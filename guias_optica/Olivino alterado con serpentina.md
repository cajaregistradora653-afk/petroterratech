## Alteracion
products of Al-poor, magnesian minerals, especially olivine, Mg-Pyroxenes, Mg-amphiboles. The most characteristic occurrence of serpentine is that derived from dunites, peridotites and other ultramafic-mafic rocks. A second occurrence of serpentine is in metamorphic contact zone in carbonate rocks, where it results from the alteration of forsterite marble.

## Confusables (revisar antes de anotar)
Olivino fresco (III orden, sin malla) vs alterado: islas de olivino + venas de serpentina (textura mesh) + magnetita.
