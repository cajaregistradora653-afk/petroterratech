# Olivino -> `Olivino_PPL` / `Olivino_XPL`

Fuente: https://www.alexstrekeisen.it/english/pluto/olivine.php

## PPL (luz polarizada plana)
- 
Form:
Six sides circular section on (001), rectangular section on (010), six or eight sides section elongated on c in (100) section.

- 
Color:
Colorless or pale green-yellow with increasing Fe.

- 
Relief:
High.

- 
Interference colors:
Strong, III order colors.

- 
Cleavage:
Rather poor on (010) and (110).

## XPL (nicoles cruzados)
- 
Interference colors:
Strong, III order colors.
