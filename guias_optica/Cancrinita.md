# Cancrinita -> `Cancrinita_PPL` / `Cancrinita_XPL`

Fuente: https://www.alexstrekeisen.it/english/pluto/cancrinite.php

## Descripcion fuente
Cancrinite pegmatite: Yellow cancrinite with black biotite, nepheline and alkali feldspar. Litchfield, Massachusetts. Photo by W. Cordua, From Wisconsin Geological and Natural History Survey .

## PPL (luz polarizada plana)
- 
Form:
Columnar or needle-shaped crystals.

- 
Cleavage:
Perpendicular to the long axis.

- 
Color:
Colorless in thin section.


## XPL (nicoles cruzados)
- 
Extinction:
Straight.

- 
Birefringence:
High, from yellow to violet.
Cancrinite pegmatite: Yellow cancrinite with black biotite, nepheline and alkali feldspar. Litchfield, Massachusetts. Photo by W. Cordua, From
Wisconsin Geological and Natural History Survey
.

## Confusables (revisar antes de anotar)
Nefelina/calcita por asociacion; verificar maclas y relieve. Guiarse por caption y roca huesped.
