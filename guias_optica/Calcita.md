# Calcita -> `Calcita_PPL` / `Calcita_XPL`

Fuente: https://www.alexstrekeisen.it/english/pluto/calcite.php

## PPL (luz polarizada plana)
- 
Color:
Colourless

- 
Form:
Usually show fine to coarse anhedral aggregate

- 
Cleavage:
Perfect rhombohedral cleavage intersect at about 75°

- 
Interference colors:
V orders interference colors

- 
Relief:
Calcite is characterized by change in relief during a 360°rotation of the stage; the crystals is said to twinkle during rotation.

## XPL (nicoles cruzados)
- 
Interference colors:
V orders interference colors


## Confusables (revisar antes de anotar)
Dolomita/plagioclasa: calcita con maclas polisinteticas finas + colores pastel de alto orden + clivaje romboedrico.
