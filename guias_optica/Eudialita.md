# Eudialita -> `Eudialita_PPL` / `Eudialita_XPL`

Fuente: https://www.alexstrekeisen.it/english/pluto/eudialyte.php

## PPL (luz polarizada plana)
- 
Color:
Colorless in thin sections.

- 
Pleochroism:
Weak, colorless to pale pink-yellow

- 
Form:
Commonly as irregular masses and vein filling.

- 
Relief:
Medium.

- 
Interference colors:
Low to very Low (pale blue-gray of I order).
Eudialyte Syenite (The red crystals). Brasil. From Kevin Walsh.

## XPL (nicoles cruzados)
-

## Confusables (revisar antes de anotar)
Nefelina/feldespatoides: eudialita con pleocroismo rosa-morado debil y zonacion en sienitas alcalinas.
